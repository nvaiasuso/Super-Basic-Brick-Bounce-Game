BLOCK BREAKER

Move with your bound left/right keys and tilt with the bound tilt keys to aim the ball. Break every outlined brick, avoid the striped killbricks, and clear all 8 levels. Dodge the nextbots' warning beams; in a battle, rotate the shield with left/right to keep the ball inside the moving ring. Hit five bricks in a row to earn an extra ball. You have 3 lives; press R after a win or game over to restart. Press Escape to pause and open the keybind menu.

Paused — Controls

Click a key button, then press a key. Escape cancels key capture; press Escape again to resume. Shift+Q toggles auto-play. Shift+Y opens the admin menu.
